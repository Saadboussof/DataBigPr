🎓  Partition Key Design — What students must justify 
Why (city, zone_id) and not just taxi_id for vehicle_positions? Answer: the API queries 'all 
vehicles in zone X', not 'all trips by taxi Y'. Partition by query pattern. 
Why date_bucket in trips? Answer: unbounded partition growth. Without bucketing by day/week, 
one partition accumulates millions of rows and causes hotspots. 
Students who copy this schema without understanding it will not survive Q&A in Week 8.
